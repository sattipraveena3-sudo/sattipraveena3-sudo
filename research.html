Research agenda

The next questions.

A focused doctoral agenda grounded in my current research and systems experience.

01 / Retrieval + LLMs

How can retrieval-grounded models become more reliable?

Explore retrieval quality, attribution, factuality, uncertainty, and system-level evaluation for knowledge-intensive language applications and agents.

02 / Multimodal + Time Series

How should models reason over asynchronous and incomplete evidence?

Study irregular multimodal signals while preserving temporal structure, uncertainty, traceability, and robust representations.

03 / Trustworthy Agents

How can autonomous AI remain verifiable and governable?

Develop agent architectures with explicit policy, sandboxed execution, human approval, independent verification, observability, and auditable evidence.
